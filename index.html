Discover Collections

Curated MDBList collections from DuckKota's guide. Pick a target collection, then add any of them as a folder on your sidebar — backed automatically by your AIOLists addon.
Each "Add" stages two changes: the list is imported into your AIOLists addon (push from the Addons tab) and a folder is added to the chosen collection (push from the Collections tab). AIOLists must be installed in this profile — if it isn't, adding will stage it for you.

Community Collections

Curated collection packs shared by the Nuvio community (snapshot from nuvio.tv). Each pack bundles ready-made collections plus the addons they need.
Adding a pack stages its collections into your Collections (push from the Collections tab) and its required addons into your addon rack (push from the Addons tab). Packs reuse the original author's configured addon URLs. Nuvio Companion tries a live remote registry first, then falls back to the bundled offline snapshot.

Catalogues

Read-only view of every catalogue advertised by your installed addons. Useful for spotting which addon is creating a mystery home-screen row.
Nuvio stores home-screen layout as an app-defined JSON blob with no documented schema, so write-back from a third-party tool isn't reliable. To remove a catalogue: use the source addon's own Configure page to disable it (then Edit URL + Push in the Addons tab), or remove the source addon entirely. To reorder: do it in the Nuvio app itself.

Collections

Profile collections stored in Nuvio's cloud. Useful for building a curated Companion Home layer from your addon catalogues.
Collections are a full-replace JSON blob per profile. Push only after checking the preview below. Switch to merge mode to update only collections with matching IDs and leave the rest untouched.
